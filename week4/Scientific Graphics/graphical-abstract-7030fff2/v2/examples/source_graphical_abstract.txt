Hyper-osmotic stress activates the membrane sensor.
The sensor is located in the plasma membrane of the cell.
Sensor activation releases the transcription factor.
The transcription factor induces its target genes.
Target gene expression increases 3.2-fold.
Induction of the target genes promotes survival.
Expression of the reporter was 1.0 under iso-osmotic and 3.2 under hyper-osmotic conditions.
