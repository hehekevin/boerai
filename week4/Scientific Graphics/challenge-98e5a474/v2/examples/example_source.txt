EGF binds EGFR at the plasma membrane.
Ligand-bound EGFR recruits GRB2.
GRB2 recruits SOS, which activates RAS.
RAS-GTP activates ERK through RAF and MEK.
Erlotinib inhibits EGFR kinase activity.
Selumetinib inhibits MEK and thereby ERK phosphorylation.
Active ERK inhibits SOS through negative feedback.
The tubular reactor is held at 350 °C.
Products pass through a cold trap at -20 °C.
Each run comprised flush (0.5 h), heat-up (1 h), hold (4 h) and cool-down (1.5 h) stages.
The actuator drives the mechanical load with 4 ms latency; the controller updates every 10 ms.
